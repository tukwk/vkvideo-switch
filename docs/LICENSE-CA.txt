CA certificate bundle
=====================

data/cacert.bin is the Mozilla CA root certificate bundle as published at
https://curl.se/ca/cacert.pem

The bundle is generated from the Mozilla CA database. The certificate data is
distributed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0 (MPL 2.0):
https://www.mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/

It is refreshed with tools/update-ca.sh.
